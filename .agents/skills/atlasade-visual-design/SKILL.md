# Atlasade visual design

## Start with the merchant's task

1. Identify the page or scene, the merchant's question, the record they need to recognize, and the action or decision the design should support. Honor the requested mode. For a planning exercise, return a proposal and verification plan. Implement only when the task authorizes implementation.
2. Find the website root and read `docs/quiet-orbit.md` and `public/quiet-orbit.css`. Review the existing implementation for the surface. Use `src/SalePortrait.jsx` and `src/day/` as references for how the River & Oak receipt links to effects without creating another transaction. Use `public/images/quiet-orbit.svg` for established symbols.
3. Establish the evidence behind the screen. Use the site's approved capability content and citations for public wording. Keep the location, record ID, lifecycle and merchant availability explicit where they affect the answer. Use synthetic data for illustrative scenes and label them visibly. Leave unverified actions, commercial claims, permissions and persistence unverified. Product screenshots need an approved interface and synthetic or approved public data.

## Compose in Quiet Orbit

- Frame the work in midnight and deep teal. Use warm cream and dark ink for the record, mint for labeled stock, and champagne for a labeled connection or cash value. Reuse token roles, the dimensional blue mark, and native reading and tab order. Keep meaningful content readable before motion runs.
- Place the subject in clear focus and give it room. Keep one readable foreground record, a quieter supporting plane, and linked outcomes at the depth appropriate to their relationship. Use consistent upper-left light, soft shadows, fine borders, and upright type. Let any rear photo or illustration establish place while preserving contrast over it.
- Preserve the editorial serif emphasis, interface typography, and short monospace identifiers. Use real text for money, item names, states and controls. Keep numeric column alignment and let text wrap naturally as the viewport narrows.
- Choose one interaction that shows a meaningful state change or relationship. Use native controls and a finite transfer of emphasis from a labeled source to a labeled destination. Draw connectors with local SVG or CSS and keep their endpoints visually attached as the layout changes. Change the meaningful DOM state immediately and let motion reinforce it. Reduced-motion users get the same end state and text without a moving trace.
- Use original Atlasade artwork, trusted product captures and browser-native effects. A mask can reveal a highlight or decorative layer on activation while headings, amounts, controls and focus outlines stay visible. Prefer CSS transitions and static SVG. Keep composition and interaction free of imported templates, third-party media, remote font or library requirements, background video and heavy rendering frameworks.

## Review the work

For visual implementation or review, capture matching before and after images at the same data state, viewport and scroll position. Inspect the screenshots and test the actual interaction. For planning, inspect available baseline screenshots and specify the future capture and measurement plan. Identify which evidence was observed and which checks await an implementation. Use [the qualification reference](references/qualification.md) for captures, accessibility and budgets.

Keep this skill, review notes and local evidence outside the Vite public directory, source imports and deployment artifacts. Keep the work within the task's publication scope.
