Sales Register

The register is where staff add items, attach a customer when useful, take payment, and complete a sale without leaving the selling flow.

A focused register keeps lines moving while still creating the records the business needs later for reporting, support, and customer follow up.

Customer/CRM

Customer/CRM links purchases to a customer profile when the shopper shares a phone number or identity at checkout.

Staff can recognize returning customers and the business can understand repeat visits without turning checkout into a back office CRM session.

Inventory

Inventory tracks physical goods and products where stock matters while allowing everyday prepared items to flow through the register normally.

Operators can protect retail stock from overselling while keeping prepared food and drink sales simple.

Payments & Recovery

Payments & Recovery helps staff finish cash and card sales, handle declined attempts, and know what to do when a payment is unclear.

Payment interruptions are stressful. The system should show the next safe action instead of leaving the register stuck.

Reporting

Reporting summarizes sales, taxes, refunds, corrections, tender activity, and customer activity for operators and owners.

Owners need a clean daily view without rebuilding totals from receipts, payment portals, and spreadsheets.

Business Operations

Business Operations covers business day status, saved tickets, manager attention, operator roles, and safe closeout behavior.

The day should close only when unresolved work is visible and understood, not silently discarded.

Guidance Assistant

The Guidance Assistant can help staff find items, understand recovery steps, answer common questions, and suggest follow up actions.

New or busy staff can get help without leaving checkout or calling a manager for every small question.

Content Studio / Social Observer

Content Studio / Social Observer helps merchants notice business signals, find content ideas, and draft marketing notes based on what is actually happening in the business.

Most merchants know they should post more consistently, but they do not have time to invent content from scratch. AtlasADE can turn sales trends, customer patterns, services, products, and seasonal signals into practical ideas while keeping the merchant in control.

Integrations

Integrations connect AtlasADE to the tools around payments, accounting, inventory, marketing, hardware, and reporting.

A merchant should not have to enter the same business facts into every tool they use.

Backup & Replay

Backup & Replay protects business records by preserving the sequence of important events and helping the system recover after restart.

A register should not lose or duplicate a sale because a device restarts, power blips, or the system needs to recover.
