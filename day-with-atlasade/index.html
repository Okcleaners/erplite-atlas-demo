A Day With Atlasade

River & Oak

Take the counter at a fictional neighborhood café. Follow one sale from the first cup to the closing count.

The interactive register uses JavaScript. The café walkthrough below is available to read.
Read the café walkthrough
  1. Begin the day. Open the River location’s register with its starting cash float.
  2. The first customer. Add a house coffee and an oat bar. The ticket calculates an illustrative sales tax.
  3. Customer recognition. Find a fictional regular and attach their profile to the ticket while you are still at the counter.
  4. Checkout. Accept simulated cash, return the change, and inspect the completed receipt.
  5. Follow the sale. The cashier sees the customer’s receipt. The operator sees one fewer oat bar at River and the original count at Hill. Finance can distinguish sales revenue from tax and trace the cash retained.
  6. Close the day. Compare the counted drawer with opening cash plus cash received less change. Resolve any difference, close the register, and replay the day.
